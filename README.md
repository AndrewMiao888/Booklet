# Small Things, Big Ocean

A 32-page illustrated interactive field guide to plastic pollution, the Great Pacific Garbage Patch, microplastics, bottled-water research, evidence and lower-waste habits.

## Open the site

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The site uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; it has no package manager, build step or external runtime dependency. Google Fonts load when an internet connection is available, with system font fallbacks for offline use.

## Explore

- Use **Open the field guide** to enter the book.
- Turn pages with the arrow buttons or keyboard arrow keys. Select a chapter pill or contents link to jump ahead.
- Save your place in this browser with **Save place**.
- Explore the scale, bottle-volume calculator, myth check, gyre, reuse choices, laundry habits, recycling guide and one-week action list.
- Open **Sources & further reading** to follow the research and public guidance.

## Evidence notes

The bottled-water page distinguishes the 2024 study's approximate average of 240,000 nano- and microplastic particles per litre from its upper observations. The high-end figure of roughly 555,000 for a 1.5-litre bottle is a volume-scaled estimate; it is not a universal count, and most particles in that study were nanoplastics. The credit-card comparison is presented as an uncertain 2019 estimate, not a measured personal weekly intake. Study links and public-health guidance are included in the reader and reference shelf.
